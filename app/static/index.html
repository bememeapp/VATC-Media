The house style

Keep the idea. Change the setting.

Only the backgrounds inside the photos are edited. Text, usernames, avatars and white space outside the photo areas come from the original file. The image editor is instructed to keep the existing subjects, composition and colour treatment.

Funny first. Informative throughout.

Captions start with a short hook, followed by detailed, readable paragraphs and natural topic keywords. English, occasional emojis, no hashtags and no page-specific promotion.

A final look makes all the difference.

Use Regenerate image for another background, or Rewrite caption for fresh wording. Each new image edit starts from your original post.
